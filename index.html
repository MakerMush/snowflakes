          Welcome to Flake Lab

          1. Design one wedgeAdd shapes from the left. Each one is cut from paper folded into this wedge.
          2. Watch it unfoldThe preview shows the whole snowflake, with every arm a mirror of the next.
          3. Print, fold, cutPrint the template, fold on the dashed lines, cut on the black lines, and unfold.

          Teaching? Kid mode has bigger buttons and fewer choices, and math mode adds symmetry activities and worksheets. Switch in More › Modes, or add ?kids or ?math to the address.
